# Frontiers GenAi Hackathon
- ALX Kigali | July 28, 2026

This hackathon asks one question: what can you build in 12 hours that addresses a real problem facing Africa today? Choose one track to build

**Disclaimer**

Use generative AI responsibly. Outputs that are baised, harmful, or misleading will result in disqualification. Consider the real impack of your tool on the communities it is designed to serve.

## Problem Areas

### Track 01

**Agriculture and BioSystems**

Prompt: Build a generative Ai tool that helps a farmer, agronomist, or food system actor make a better decision about what to grow, how to protect livestock, or when and where to sell produce.

Example Ideas:

- Identify a crop disease from a photo and recommend a treatment in the farmer's language
- Give planting and harvesting advice based on a farmer's location and current season

### Track 02

**Health Sciences and Biotech**

Prompt: Build a generative AI tool that improves how patients, cargivers, or health workers communicate, understand, or act on health information without replacing the clinician.

Example Ideas:

- Translate a medical diagnosis or discharge summary into an actionable explanation for patient
- Detect counterfeit and expired medicine by scanning a package and checking against known identifiers

### Track 03

**AI and Consumer Technology**

Prompt: Build an AI product designed specifically for a user who speaks an African language, uses a low-end device, or has unreliable internet access.

Example Ideas:

- Build a question-answering tool that works accurately in Kinyarwanda, Swahili, or another African language

- Create a credit assessment tool for informal workers using mobile money history and community references instead of a credit file

### Track 04

**Energy and Climate infrastructure**

Prompt: Build a generative AI tool that helps a household, community or local official produce, manage, or adapt energy and climate conditions.

Example Ideas:

- Optimize power distribution across a solar microgrid so that critical households nad facilities are prioritized during shortages

- Guide a community through documenting and submitting a carbon credit claim for conservation work already underway

### Track 05

**Industrial Systems and Sovereign Technology**

Prompt: Build a generative AI product that helps an African manufacturer, engineer, or business owner make, inspect, or deliver something with higher value than raw materials.

Example Ideas:

- Analyze satelite or aerial imagery to identify a resource location, infrastructure gap, or land boundary for local planning

- Help an engineer adapt a product design to use material and manufacturing methods available locally.


## Bonus: Design with Ethics in Mind

Teams will receive extra credit in judging for including one or more of the following:

- human approval step
- evaluation metrics
- personalization with constraints
