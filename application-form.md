
# Frontiers PAIO Hackathon July 28th

Join The Changemakers Convening and Breakthrough Ventures and The African Olympiad Academy for an engaging in-person generative AI hackathon with Google DeepMind. We are gathering 200+ students at Norrsken House in Kigali, Rwanda during the Pan African Informatics Olympiad for 12 hours of prototyping, building, and developing leveraging generative AI tools such as Google Veo3 and Nano Banana. 
This Hackathon is open to PAIO participants and both undergraduate and graduate students. Top teams will win cash prizes. Please fill out the brief intake form below.

Your responses will help us understand your background, interests, and motivations, and will be used to determine acceptance into the event. Please answer each question thoughtfully.

Please note that accepted applicants will have a profile created in our Event Directory (including name, bio, and project interests). This directory is a key tool for sponsor networking and team formation. By submitting this application, you agree to these details.
